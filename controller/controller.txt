Mode Button - Iterates through the 4 different modes. 
Select - Creates save point (point of interest).
Next - Goes to next save point. 
Back - Goes to previous save point 
Reset - Removes all saved points. Resets image to mandlebrot, coords (0,0), x step 3, maxiter 64.
Joystick push - Goes to (0,0) at current zoom and iteration no.
(Pallette - Iterates through colour pallete options - not yet implemented)
